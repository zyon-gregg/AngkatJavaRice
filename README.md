Angkat is a database management system aiming to digitalize the business workflows of fish ports and related businesses in the fish industry, improving the pen and paper listing system. Our system, the Angkat Fish Port Management System, digitalizes the daily operations of a fish port, where catch is landed, sorted, priced, stored, and sold.
